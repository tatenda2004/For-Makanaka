01 — The letter

Makanah❤️,

There’s something a little strange about saying goodbye to someone I never got to meet in person.

We never got the simple memories — a random conversation that lasted too long, laughing at something nobody else understood, or one ordinary day that somehow became a day worth remembering.

And yet, here we are. You’re leaving for Australia, and I still wanted you to know that someone here is genuinely wishing you well.

I hope this new chapter surprises you in the best ways. I hope you find places that feel like home, people who make you laugh, and opportunities that take you further than you imagined.

Don't be afraid of the unfamiliar. Sometimes the biggest chapters begin with the scariest first step.

And who knows? Maybe “never met” is only a temporary sentence.

— Tatenda 🤍
