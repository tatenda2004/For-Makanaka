01 — From Dickson

Dear Mia,

It's strange writing a farewell to someone I never got to meet in person.

We never got the ordinary things — sitting together, laughing about something random, taking a picture, or making memories we could look back on.

But somehow, you still became someone I was genuinely glad to know.

Now you're going to Australia. A new country. New people. New places. A completely new chapter.

I hope it is kind to you. I hope you find people who make you feel at home, places that make you stop and smile, and opportunities you never saw coming.

And maybe “never met” doesn't have to mean “never will.”

Take care of yourself, Mia. Wherever life takes you, I hope it takes you somewhere beautiful.
— Dickson
